Smart Capturer Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Smart Capturer is a private household tool for saving images, links, notes, and related metadata.

Data handled by the app

The app processes the captures you submit, the descriptive metadata you enter, and a household access code used to protect its upload and analysis endpoints. The access code is stored on your device and sent to the Smart Capturer server when you use those endpoints.

Storage and sharing

Captures are stored in the configured private Google Drive folder. Google Cloud Storage may be used as a fallback inbox. Smart Capturer requests the Google Drive drive.file permission, which limits it to files used or created by this app. Data is not sold or used for advertising.

Optional AI analysis

If AI analysis is enabled by the household administrator, a capture may be sent to the configured AI provider to suggest a title, category, context, tags, and destination. AI analysis can be disabled without affecting capture storage.

Retention and deletion

The household controls retention through its Google Drive and Google Cloud accounts. Deleting a capture from those storage services removes it from Smart Capturer's storage.
